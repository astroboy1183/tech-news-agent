Ink & Paper

A broadsheet in the dark. Ink is the default — near-black with a single warm gold accent. Paper is the daylight mode. Layout, spacing and type scale never change between them.

Why no colour per section. Beyond about six, categorical colours stop separating reliably for colourblind readers — that was checked, not guessed. Rather than cap the portal at six, identity moves to the section name, its place in the nav, and the page it sits on. Colour is then free to mean one thing only: this is worth your attention.
