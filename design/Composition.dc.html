Editorial system

How the front page composes itself

Nobody edits this page. Every slot is filled by a rule, so the rules have to be good — and inspectable. This is the part that decides whether the portal reads as edited or as a script that ranked some rows.

Choosing the lead — five gates, all must pass EVALUATED EVERY 10 MINUTES
01
Corroborated by 3+ independent sources. A single outlet, however good, is never the lead. This alone kills most press-release noise.
02
Has an AI summary. If it wasn't worth spending on, it isn't worth 36px. Ties the lead to the budget's own judgement.
03
Under 6 hours old, or still gaining sources. Velocity, not just count — a story picking up its 4th and 5th outlet right now beats one that had 8 yesterday.
04
Not the same section as yesterday's lead, unless it clears the threshold by 25%. Stops AI & ML holding the lead every single day on volume.
05
Not muted by your preferences. The weekly preference pass can demote a whole topic out of lead contention.

And a manual pin overrides all five. One click on any story pins it as the lead for up to 12 hours. Automation gets it wrong sometimes; the fix should take three seconds, not a deploy. The pin shows a small marker so you know the page isn't running on rules right now.

Headline stress test REAL HEADLINES ARE 24–190 CHARACTERS

Placeholder copy is always a tidy 70 characters and always looks good. These are the shapes that actually break a front page. The lead box is fixed at three lines with a min-height, so the standfirst never moves.

24 CHARS · SHORT Linux 6.19 is out BOX HOLDS · STANDFIRST STAYS PUT
188 CHARS · OVERLONG Researchers at three institutions publish a joint paper describing a method for training vision-language models that reduces labelled-data requirements by roughly eighty percent CLAMPED AT 3 LINES · NO OVERFLOW
COLON + LONG COMPOUND TOKEN CVE-2026-31847: unauthenticated remote code execution in ConnectWise ScreenConnect versions before 25.4.2 WRAPS CLEANLY · NO HORIZONTAL SCROLL
ALL CAPS + EM DASH EXCLUSIVE — NVIDIA'S NEXT DATACENTRE PART SLIPS TWO QUARTERS, ACCORDING TO THREE SUPPLY-CHAIN SOURCES NORMALISED TO SENTENCE CASE ON INGEST

Ingest normalisation, before anything is stored: strip the outlet name where feeds append it, collapse ALL-CAPS to sentence case, convert straight quotes to typographic, drop trailing ellipses, and trim leading tags like EXCLUSIVE — into a badge instead of the headline.

When there is nothing to lead with
NO STORY CLEARS THE GATES The lead slot collapses and the four-across row promotes to the top. The page never shows a weak story at 36px.
A SECTION IS QUIET Its block shows what it has and says “3 stories today” rather than padding to five. Quiet days are information.
NO IMAGE IN THE FEED The thumbnail slot is dropped entirely, not filled with a grey box — text reflows to full width.
